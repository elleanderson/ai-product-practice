# AI Product Practice

Working prompt patterns for product work, by [Elle Anderson](https://andersonco.uk).

Each pattern is written the way I actually use it: the prompt skeleton, the rules that make it trustworthy, and the documented failure modes, because a prompt without its failure modes is a demo, not a practice.

## What is here

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| [prompt-patterns/discovery-synthesis.md](prompt-patterns/discovery-synthesis.md) | Turns a pile of interview debriefs into observations, patterns, contradictions and decisions without letting the model invent your customers |
| [prompt-patterns/prd-critique-panel.md](prompt-patterns/prd-critique-panel.md) | Puts a PRD in front of a panel of critics with different incentives, so the gaps show before the review meeting does |
| [assessments/ai-era-pm-capability-assessment.md](assessments/ai-era-pm-capability-assessment.md) | A diagnostic for where a product person actually sits between having opinions about AI and shipping with it daily, scored on the last sixty days of behaviour |

More patterns land here as they earn their place. A pattern earns its place by being used on real work at least three times and surviving.

Installable versions of several of these methods, for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other agents, live in [product-leader-skills](https://github.com/elleanderson/product-leader-skills).
